Restoring “Remove Ads” · 恢复「去除广告」

If you bought Remove Ads and ads appear again after reinstalling or switching devices, open the gear menu → Remove Ads → Restore while signed in with the same Apple ID.

买过「去除广告」后重装或换了设备又出现广告：用同一个 Apple ID 登录，打开齿轮菜单 →「去除广告」→「恢复购买」。
