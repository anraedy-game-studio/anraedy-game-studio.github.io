Privacy Policy

Ammo Match3 TD · Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how Ammo Match3 TD (the “Game”), made by anraedy game studio (“we”, “us”), handles information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Your progress, records and settings are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete the Game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the Game shows interstitial ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect device information such as your IP address, device model, operating system, ad interactions and, only if you allow it, your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA).

The first time the Game starts, iOS asks whether the Game may track your activity. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared and you may see less personalised ads. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google’s use of this data is governed by its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you buy “Remove Ads”, the Game no longer loads the AdMob SDK and no ad data is sent.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The Game only receives confirmation from Apple of whether “Remove Ads” has been purchased, and stores that status on your device.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

隐私政策

弹药三消塔防 · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 15 日

本政策说明 anraedy game studio（「我们」）开发的《弹药三消塔防》（「本游戏」）如何处理信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

我们不收集、不保存、也不出售任何个人信息。本游戏没有账号、不需要登录，也没有我们自己的服务器。游戏进度、记录和设置只保存在你的设备上，删除游戏后一并清除。

2. 广告（Google AdMob）

本游戏的免费版会显示 Google AdMob 提供的插屏广告。为了投放和统计广告，AdMob 可能收集设备信息，例如 IP 地址、设备型号、系统版本、广告互动情况，以及仅在你允许时收集设备的广告标识符（IDFA）。

首次启动时，iOS 会询问是否允许本游戏跟踪你的活动。选择「要求 App 不跟踪」后，IDFA 不会被共享，你看到的广告个性化程度可能会降低。你可以随时在「设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 跟踪」中更改。

Google 对这些数据的使用适用其自身政策：Google 隐私权政策，以及Google 如何使用来自使用其服务的网站或应用的信息。

购买「去除广告」后，本游戏不再加载 AdMob，也不会发送任何广告数据。

3. 应用内购买

购买完全由苹果通过 App Store 处理，我们不会接触你的支付信息。本游戏只从苹果获知你是否已购买「去除广告」，并把这个状态保存在你的设备上。

4. 儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

5. 政策变更

我们可能不时更新本政策，更新后会发布在本页面，并修改「最后更新」日期。

6. 联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，请发邮件至 CONTACT_EMAIL。
